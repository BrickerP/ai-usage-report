# Step 2 — Search and landscape

## References studied

These references contribute principles only. No layout, artwork, character, brand, palette, or signature interaction is to be copied.

| Reference | What it solves | Pain or limitation | What to borrow | What we will not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [GitHub Skyline](https://github.blog/changelog/2024-12-09-github-skyline-cli-extension/) | Turns contribution history into a spatial, personalized artifact | Geometry is impressive but not a direct daily exploration interface | Let the actual history generate a recognizable world | Copy the 3D skyline, contribution semantics, or physical-print framing |
| [Spotify Wrapped 2025](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-12-03/2025-wrapped-user-experience/) | Converts personal data into playful, revisitable stories | Story cards and controls can become a long presentation layer | Strong personalization, recognizable traits, direct revisit of moments | A slideshow, invented personality label, share-first funnel, or auto-advancing story |
| [WakaTime embeds and API](https://wakatime.com/developers) | Publishes precise coding history with safe public embeds and daily summaries | Conventional charts remain useful but visually generic | Exact values, public-safe history, daily drilldown, honest unavailable/stale states | A standard dashboard, leaderboard, or API-key-dependent client |
| [Codex Resets](https://codex-resets.com/) | Gives a narrow live-data premise a memorable identity | The charm depends on one joke and a compact status, not reusable game mechanics | One coherent premise and low-friction return visits | Its copy, layout, status-card treatment, or visual trade dress |
| [Steam Replay community feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/discussions/forum/7/694247829077510142/) | Shows the appeal of a personal game-history recap | Users call out percentages without exact totals and ask for longer historical context | Keep exact numbers adjacent to playful summaries and retain All time | Percent-only traits, opaque genre labels, annual-only history, or unverifiable classification |
| Manual training log / speedrun splits | Makes progress tangible with dates, splits, personal best, and current endpoint | Manual upkeep and flat tables are not visually distinctive | First seen, personal record, and current endpoint as truthful checkpoints | Failure, global competition, medals, or fabricated target times |

## Trend synthesis

1. **Personal data is becoming an artifact, not merely a dashboard.** Skyline spatializes history; Wrapped packages it as a story; public embeds make it portable.
2. **The strongest experiences are unmistakably personal.** A generic animation is weaker than a world whose geometry changes with the underlying history.
3. **Traits need evidence.** A playful label becomes distrusted when exact totals, time windows, or classification reasons are hidden.
4. **Revisit should not mean restart.** Direct access to a moment is more useful than replaying an onboarding sequence.
5. **All-time context is a differentiator.** Annual recaps are familiar, but this product's value is a continuously growing personal archive.

## Interaction patterns worth borrowing

- Data-derived spatial form from GitHub Skyline.
- One coherent, personal narrative from Wrapped and Codex Resets.
- Precise daily drilldown and public-data discipline from WakaTime.
- Personal-best / checkpoint language from manual training logs.
- Direct revisiting of any moment instead of a forced linear presentation.

## Traps to avoid

- A fixed animation with data shown only after it ends.
- Traits based on vendor marketing, token volume as quality, or cost as efficiency.
- Multiple calls to action for Enter, Reveal, Replay, and Change Route.
- A second archive or results card repeating Hero and chart facts.
- A Canvas-only game that loses URLs, text, keyboard semantics, and accessible exact tables.
- Scroll interception, fake physics, collision, lives, scores, badges, or rewards.

## Updated positioning

> **Run the Archive is a continuously growing personal AI history where every focused model becomes a deterministic, traversable 2D level generated from its real recorded dates and usage pattern.**

Unlike a coding dashboard, the history is spatial and playable. Unlike a yearly recap, it remains All time and continuously updates. Unlike a game-themed portfolio, its map, signature, record, and endpoint are all traceable to exact published values.

## Design decision carried forward

- The chart and level become one surface.
- A model change is the route change; no new route controls are added.
- Run Signature is a single history-derived trait with visible evidence.
- Record is a real landmark encountered during traversal, not a hidden result behind a button.
- The journey continues to Last Seen / Now, avoiding the false impression that usage stopped at the record.
